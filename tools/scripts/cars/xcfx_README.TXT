Xylocaine XF:
-------------
By Crazy Jimmy


This car is basically a modified Lamborghini Countach. I wanted to create a more 'race' inspired Lambo and after countless trail-and-errors, we finally have the Xylocaine XF.

Xylocaine means: Xylocaine (lidocaine) is the most widely used local anaesthetic in dental procedures, used for local infiltration, minor and major nerve blocks.

As you can see, the interior is completely reworked, the 'race' inspired theme is carried out through the blocked off air-vents and custom gauges, the rest is for you to discover.

4 Colours are available:

Dakar Yellow
Duplode Purple
Barbados Green
And a more 'race' inspired Green paint-job
