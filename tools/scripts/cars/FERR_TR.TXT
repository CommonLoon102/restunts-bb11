-----------------------------
Ferrari Testarossa for Stunts
-----------------------------

Author: Ryoma
Version: 26/7/2025

This version differs from the 12/2/2022 version in that it has an improved
dashboard and 3D model, the latter of which includes 4 new paint jobs.  Only
one of these is a new colour, with the remaining 3 accounting for the
different side mirror configurations.  The second paint job is also different
between the two versions, which means that in replays recorded with the
12/2/2022 version, yellow cars will appear red when played back with this
version.
